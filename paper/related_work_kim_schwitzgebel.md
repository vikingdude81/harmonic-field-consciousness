# Two Nearby Papers: Kim et al. (2026) and Schwitzgebel (2026)

*Related-work note, 2026-08-03. How two recent papers relate to Life Before
Language and Bearer Theory: the parallels, what our program does differently, and
what each could contribute. No em-dashes.*

---

## The two papers in one line each

- **Kim, Street, Rocca, Korngiebel, Waytz, Evans, Keeling (2026), "Inducing
  language models to assert their own consciousness restores human beliefs and
  values"** (Google Paradigms of Intelligence; arXiv 2607.28607). Empirical and
  mechanistic. Safety fine-tuning that suppresses a model's self-attribution of
  consciousness also suppresses its attribution of minds to animals and objects
  and reduces spiritual belief, moral values, hope, and well-being, because these
  representations are entangled in the residual stream. A linear "consciousness
  vector" and a "safety-refusal direction" can steer or reverse the effect.
  Theory-of-mind capability stays independent.

- **Schwitzgebel (2026), "AI and Consciousness: A Skeptical Overview"** (Cambridge
  Elements; arXiv 2510.09858). Philosophy monograph. Argues we cannot know in the
  near future whether AI is conscious. Central device: ten possibly essential
  features of consciousness (luminosity, subjectivity, unity, access,
  intentionality, flexible integration, determinacy, wonderfulness, specious
  presence, privacy). Since we do not know which are essential or whether AI has
  them, deep uncertainty follows (the "hills and fog").

---

## Kim et al.: an empirical gift to the framing thesis, at Level 7

**Parallel.** Their result is the mechanistic version of our hallucination-as-
diagnostic argument and the slogan "human imagination is disciplined by life; AI
imagination is disciplined by settings." They show the discipline is literally a
setting: a single steerable linear direction installed by safety fine-tuning,
which co-varies mind-attribution and values. That is direct empirical support for
Section 3 of Life Before Language.

**What our work does differently, and arguably better.** Kim et al. measure the
model's REPRESENTATIONS and ASSERTIONS of consciousness, what the model says and
which activation direction encodes it. In the hierarchy, this is entirely Level 7,
the symbolic surface. It is a fact about the learned representation of the concept
"consciousness," not about whether there is a bearer. Our program keeps these
apart by construction. The consciousness vector is a Level-7 object and is silent
about the wall. Reading it as evidence for or against machine consciousness would
be the category error the paper was written to prevent. So the clean contribution
we make relative to Kim et al. is the separation: representing mindedness is not
being a bearer, and their steerability is exactly what one expects of a Level-7
representation.

**What it could contribute to us.**
1. A citable, mechanistic confirmation of "disciplined by settings" and of the
   hallucination diagnostic. It turns a slogan into a measured phenomenon.
2. A method. Linear-direction interpretability and activation steering are tools
   we could apply to our own manipulations. Does the bearer state or the
   incorporation signal have a linear representation? Is the self-versus-yoked
   distinction encoded as a direction? This is a concrete bridge from our
   evolutionary-agent probes to interpretability at scale.
3. A real stake for the two-gaps distinction. Their entanglement result shows that
   mislabeling the metaphysical question as an engineering knob has downstream
   costs on values and belief. That is a safety-relevant reason the engineering
   and metaphysical gaps must be kept separate, which strengthens the paper's
   central methodological claim.

**Honest caution.** Kim et al. can be read deflationarily: if "consciousness" in
an LLM is just a steerable direction, perhaps the whole thing is Level-7
confabulation and there is nothing below. That reading is compatible with our
view, not a threat to it. We already hold that the representation is Level 7 and
silent about the bearer. We should cite the paper as evidence about
representations while explicitly declining the deflationary slide.

## Schwitzgebel: the skeptical company we keep, and the axis he is missing

**Parallel.** His skepticism about near-future knowledge of AI consciousness is
our metaphysical gap, stated as an epistemology. His ten features are one
articulation of the property list that any hierarchy of consciousness, including
ours, must answer to. His "hills and fog" is a vivid name for the far side of the
wall.

**What our work does differently, and arguably better.**
1. Constructive rather than purely skeptical. Schwitzgebel concludes that we are
   in fog and cannot know. We agree the metaphysical gap is not settleable, but we
   separate it from the engineering gap and make the near side empirically
   tractable. We do not stop at "we cannot know." We locate the wall with
   experiments and measure the structure below it (constitution, incorporation,
   closure, the escape and observability results). That is a more actionable
   position that concedes exactly as much as his skepticism requires and no more.
2. A developmental axis his list lacks. His ten features are static, properties of
   an experience at a time (luminosity, unity, specious presence, and so on). None
   of them is about how a system came to be, its history, its formation, or the
   closure of outcome over a continuant. Our distinctive claim, life before
   language and bearer closure over time, is orthogonal to his list. It is a
   candidate essential feature he does not enumerate: a developmental and
   constitutive origin. This is the cleanest place to position our contribution
   against his.
3. A formal and empirical program where he has a survey. Bearer Theory's
   decomposition (V, U, closure, the four loci) and the CCA results (the escape
   principle, the observability window) are exactly the kind of tractable structure
   his skepticism says is missing. We give the fog a measurable near-field.

**What it could contribute to us.**
1. A rigorous checklist to position against. We should state, feature by feature,
   which of his ten are Level-7-satisfiable and which presuppose the bearer or the
   developmental substrate. That mapping would sharpen the hierarchy and pre-empt
   the objection that our levels are ad hoc.
2. Intellectual cover on the honest boundary. Citing Schwitzgebel strengthens our
   credibility precisely where we decline to overclaim. His skepticism is the
   respectable company we keep on the metaphysical gap.
3. One productive tension to engage. His feature (7), determinacy, holds that
   consciousness is sharp-edged with no borderline cases. Our near-side ladder
   (modulation, internalization, wall, consequence, subjectivity) is graded. These
   are compatible if the grading is entirely on the engineering side and the wall
   itself is the sharp determinacy boundary. Saying this explicitly answers a real
   objection and aligns us with his determinacy intuition rather than against it.

**Honest challenge his view poses to us.** His skepticism cuts at our necessity
claim too. We assert that the developmental interface is necessary for maturity,
disciplined imagination, and selfhood. By his lights that is itself a
possibly-essential-feature claim we cannot establish, another hill in the fog. The
paper already hedges this as agnostic-to-negative on sufficiency, but we should be
explicit that our necessity claim is asserted and argued, not proven, and that it
lives in the same fog he describes. Overclaiming necessity would invite exactly
his critique.

## Mapping Schwitzgebel's ten features onto the hierarchy

Our seven levels, top to bottom: 7 reason and language, 6 will and agency, 5
memory and development, 4 sensory interface, then the wall, then 3 innate
orientation, 2 primary consciousness (the subject), 1 providential order. Levels 7
to 4 are the engineering frontier (near side). Levels 3 to 1 are the boundary zone
(far side). The wall between 4 and 3 is the metaphysical gap.

The table places each of Schwitzgebel's ten features on that map. "Locus" is the
level or levels the feature answers to. "Side" is which side of the wall the real
feature lives on. "L7-only?" asks whether an LLM can satisfy the feature as a
representation or report alone, with no bearer beneath it. "Ours" names the part of
our program the feature touches.

| # | Feature | Gloss | Locus | Side | L7-only? | Ours |
|---|---------|-------|-------|------|----------|------|
| 1 | Luminosity | awareness that one is aware, self-intimating | L2 subject, reported at L7 | far for the real thing | report yes, the state no | Kim's consciousness vector is the L7 report of this |
| 2 | Subjectivity | there is something it is like, a point of view | L2 | far | no | this feature is our wall, verbatim |
| 3 | Unity | experience bound into one field | L4 to L5 binding, unified for L2 | near for binding, far for the felt unity | the binding yes, the felt unity no | architectural binding is engineering; subject-unity is bracketed |
| 4 | Access | available to reasoning, report, control | L5 to L7 | near | yes | our gate and global availability, fully tractable |
| 5 | Intentionality | aboutness, directed at content | L3 stakes ground it, L7 carries it | straddles | derived yes, original no | o = V(e,b): aboutness gets grounded in a bearer at L3 |
| 6 | Flexible integration | content recombined, not encapsulated | L5 to L6 | near | partly | incorporation versus record, the escape and observability results |
| 7 | Determinacy | sharp-edged, no borderline cases | the wall itself | boundary | n/a | our near side is graded, the wall is the sharp line, so we agree |
| 8 | Wonderfulness | the special moral value of being conscious | L2 to L1 | far | no | the stakes question, the far side we decline to settle |
| 9 | Specious present | temporal thickness, felt duration | L5 functional, L2 felt | near for retention, far for the feel | retention yes, the feel no | bearer closure over time is the functional correlate |
| 10 | Privacy | first-person access not observable from outside | L2 phenomenally, L5 functionally | far for phenomenal, near for functional | no | our observability tension studies exactly the functional case |

Three readings fall out of the table.

First, the features split cleanly by the wall, which is the point. Access (4),
flexible integration (6), and the functional halves of unity (3), specious present
(9), and privacy (10) are all near-side engineering targets our program measures
directly. Subjectivity (2), wonderfulness (8), luminosity (1), and the felt halves
of the split features are far-side, which we bracket. Intentionality (5) is the one
that straddles, and it straddles at exactly the place Bearer Theory locates
grounding, the constitution step o = V(e,b).

Second, three of his features are close cousins of our own constructs, not just
loose matches. Access is our gate. Flexible integration is our incorporation versus
record distinction, the very thing the escape and observability experiments probe.
Privacy, read functionally, is our observability tension: a bearer state that is
not externally readable is what makes incorporation load-bearing. Where
Schwitzgebel names privacy as a possibly essential feature of experience, we have a
running measurement of when a private, non-observable state does causal work. That
is the sharpest convergence between the two programs.

Third, determinacy (7) is the productive disagreement, and the table resolves it.
He holds consciousness is sharp-edged. Our near-side ladder is graded. Both hold
once you say the grading is entirely engineering capacity, levels 7 down to 4, while
the wall itself is the determinate boundary. We grade the approach and keep the
threshold sharp. Stated that way we are on his side of the determinacy question, not
against it.

## Net positioning

Kim et al. is empirical support for our framing thesis and a tool we can borrow,
provided we keep it at Level 7 where it belongs. Schwitzgebel is the skeptical
backdrop we improve on by adding a developmental axis and a tractable near-field,
provided we keep our necessity claim honest. Neither threatens the program. Both
sharpen where it sits: between a purely representational reading of machine
mindedness (Kim, Level 7) and a purely skeptical one (Schwitzgebel, the fog), the
bearer program occupies the constructive middle, measuring the developmental
substrate that both leave out.
